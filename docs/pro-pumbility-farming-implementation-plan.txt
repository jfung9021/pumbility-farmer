Status: implemented and verified locally on November 23, 2026. Three subagents
implemented the engine, capture/build pipeline, and UI; API integration and
end-to-end verification are complete. Current requirements: overall-only
recommendations and UI; an adaptive
+100/+150/+200 peer window with lower-Pumbility backfill to ten peers; the 50th
percentile truncated to its earned grade; and explicit score-based MG/SG/PG
assumptions. Overall Pumbility remains the sole skill metric. All recommendation
inputs are Phoenix2-only: rankings, peer scores, chart catalog, personal bests,

1. Feature contract

Add a Pro Farming tab to Recommendations. A player qualifies when their current
official Phoenix2 overall Pumbility rank is 1 through 300 inclusive. The skill
value for this tab is the official overall Pumbility itself.

For a selected player with overall Pumbility P, exclude their official player ID
and select peers using this decision flow:

  [P, P + 100] -- at least 10 peers? --> use everyone in that window
        | fewer than 10
        v
  [P, P + 150] -- at least 10 peers? --> use everyone in that window
        | fewer than 10
        v
  [P, P + 200] -- at least 10 peers? --> use everyone in that window
        | fewer than 10
        v
  Retain those peers, then fill downward through players below P until the
  cohort contains 10 other players.

Every window includes both endpoints. Stop at the first window containing at
least ten peers; do not trim a qualifying window to ten. The backfill stage adds
only the number needed to reach ten, starting with the nearest lower Pumbility
and continuing downward. Resolve equal-rating cutoff ties by official overall
rank, then official player ID for deterministic selection. This is the meaning
of "fill downwards and backwards": add the closest lower-rated players rather
than extending the upper boundary beyond P + 200.

Use the full official ranking response as the peer population. Only recipients
have the top-300 gate; backfill can include players below overall rank 300.
Exclude self throughout and deduplicate by official player ID. If the complete
available population has fewer than ten other players, retain those available
and report limited support instead of duplicating or inventing peers.

For example, the overall number-one player starts with no upward peers and is
backfilled with the next ten lower-Pumbility players. The final cohort is shared
by Singles and Doubles suggestions. Window selection is based on player count,
not on the number of observed scores for an individual chart.

Recommend current-catalog Single charts at level >=24 and Double charts at
level >=25. The floors apply to current official levels, not fitted difficulty.
The Pro UI has one combined Overall list containing both kinds of chart, with
S/D chart badges identifying their type. It has no Overall/Singles/Doubles/Co-op
mode selector or mode-specific list. Overall Pumbility, official overall rank,
selected cohort, and marginal overall Top 50 gain are the displayed metrics.

For each eligible chart:
  - Keep one best current official raw score per qualifying peer/chart.
  - Compute an unweighted, linearly interpolated 50th percentile (median) from observed
    peer scores: h=(n-1)*0.50, interpolating between floor(h) and ceil(h).
  - Truncate to the lower boundary of the earned Phoenix2 grade. For example,
    993,400 becomes SSS at 990,000; 995,000 remains SSS+ at 995,000.
  - Select the assumed plate from the raw percentile score before grade
    truncation: exactly 1,000,000 => PG; strictly above 995,000 and below
    1,000,000 => SG; everything else => MG. See section 5 for boundary behavior.
  - Convert the truncated grade and assumed plate to chart Pumbility using the
    current Phoenix2 formula and its two-decimal truncation.
  - Calculate marginal gain to the player's shared Singles + Doubles Top 50.
  - Keep positive gains and sort descending, with deterministic chart ties.

Keep the raw percentile available alongside the truncated grade target and
assumed plate so the estimate is explainable. Record the chosen window, whether
lower-rated backfill was needed, the number added, and the actual cohort rating
range. Do not interpret an 50th percentile as an 50% success chance.

3. Coverage and source policy

Official chart boards are censored to their published leaderboard population.
A missing peer score is unknown, not zero or a failure. The available API does
not establish a complete score history for every peer. Consequently, a percentile
of observed board scores cannot be presented as the exact percentile of every
cohort member's scores when coverage is incomplete.

Report observed chart sample count / total peer count and board cap status,
alongside the chosen +100/+150/+200 stage and any downward backfill. Ten cohort
members do not guarantee ten recorded scores on every chart. Do not silently
rerun the window flow per chart or impute missing scores. No peers or no observed
chart scores means no target; sparse chart samples remain visibly limited data.
A strict requirement for all peer scores would need an additional complete
official source or suppression of incomplete charts; that policy remains a
product limitation to resolve.

Use only current Phoenix2 data. Rankings and peer scores come from official
mix=Phoenix2 responses, and chart metadata comes from the Phoenix2 catalog.
Reject Phoenix1 or mixed-version recommendation inputs. Do not use Phoenix1
scores, converted Phoenix1 scores, cross-version maximums, or Phoenix1 fallback
records when a Phoenix2 result is absent. The existing comparison feature's
Phoenix/Phoenix2 merge is outside this feature's source policy. Its roster also
requires 200 recorded scores, which is not this feature's eligibility rule.

4. Automatic PIUScores identity mapping

Live Swagger and actual responses confirm both PIUScores player profiles and
official ranking rows expose the same full gameTag, including its discriminator.
The schema explicitly documents this join; official playerId and PIUScores
account ID are separate identifiers.

Join exact full gameTag during consented-player refresh and save a dedicated
private link from the existing player key/account ID to official playerId.
Record source generation, match time and status. Reuse the saved PIUScores
selection to open the Pro tab without another username prompt.

Only resolve an automatic match when the official identity is unambiguous.
Several PIUScores accounts may map to one official player; do not enforce reverse
one-to-one uniqueness. Verify eligibility from official rank, not PIUScores'
estimatedPumbilityRank. Avoid username similarity matching.

The read-only probe found 1,059 readable profiles, 860 with full tags. Forty-five
profiles match 44 distinct current top-300 players. All matched profiles are
public; current official top-300 tags have no blanks or duplicates. These counts
do not imply that every top-300 player has a readable PIUScores account.

For missing/stale tags, a one-time official-player selection can be stored in
this browser, or the UI can direct the player to an official PIUScores import.
A manual association supports recommendations; it is not account ownership
verification. Missing personal history is handled by official reconstruction.

Generic private score snapshots intentionally exclude gameTag. Keep the join in
a separate private artifact, expose only the resolved public name/rank/total,
and retain the existing player-selection privacy boundary.

5. Personal baseline, plates, and gain

Peer scores must be official Phoenix2-only. Select a baseline automatically for
every resolved official profile, including players without a PIUScores account:

  a. Prefer a complete PIUScores API read containing at least 50 usable distinct
     current Phoenix2 Singles/Doubles charts. Preserve actual plates and chart
     Pumbility. The summed Top 50 must reach the official published total within
     one cent; a lower total indicates an incomplete import even when the API
     read itself succeeded. A newer personal total above the weekly total is
     usable. Never interpret a zero-score import as an actual empty score list.
  b. Otherwise reconstruct the player's Top 50 from their official profile's
     observed, unsupplemented raw scores. Apply the MG/SG/PG guesses below and
     current Phoenix2 chart levels, deduplicate charts, and select the highest
     50 estimated chart values. Use all current Singles/Doubles levels, not just
     S24+/D25+; those floors apply only to recommendations.
  c. Record the baseline source, whether it is estimated, fallback reason,
     observed score count, contributing Top 50, total, and 50th-chart cutoff.
     Show guessed plates as assumed, including on displayed current scores.
  d. If neither source provides 50 usable charts, show gain unavailable. Missing
     leaderboard observations are unknown slots, never zero-Pumbility slots.

An official reconstruction is an estimate because plates and unlisted scores
are unknown. Show its total beside the actual overall Pumbility and the signed
difference. Do not force the reconstructed values to sum to the official total.
Calculate marginal gain as the change within the selected baseline, using its
current chart value or its 50th-chart cutoff. Keep source dates visible. Do not
pass merged Phoenix1/Phoenix2 scores into any part of this calculation.

Use these fixed plate assumptions, evaluated on the raw, full-precision 50th
percentile q before truncating its grade:

  Raw percentile score q                 Assumed plate
  q = 1,000,000                          PG (Perfect Game)
  995,000 < q < 1,000,000                 SG (Superb Game)
  0 <= q <= 995,000                       MG (Marvelous Game)

Check the exact-million exception first. "Above 995k" is strictly greater than:
exactly 995,000 uses MG. A fractional percentile of 995,000.25 uses SG, and
999,999.99 is still SG, not PG. A score of exactly 1,000,000 uses SSS+ with PG.
Apply the plate rule before grade truncation, since all these upper scores have
the same SSS+ lower grade boundary of 995,000. Preserve both raw percentile and
grade-boundary score in the response; when PG is assumed, display 1,000,000 as
the required score so the UI does not imply PG is achievable at the grade cutoff.

These rules apply to projected targets and to reconstructed official baselines.
Preserve real recorded plates and Pumbility when using a usable PIUScores baseline.
Label reconstruction values, guessed plates, targets and gains estimated.

Marginal gain follows the existing shared-top-50 rules:
  - Existing Top 50 chart: max(0, target value - current chart value).
  - New Top 50 entrant with a full list: max(0, target value - 50th value).
  - Fewer than 50 usable scores in both sources: gain is unavailable; do not use
    the existing helper's empty-slot behavior for an incomplete imported history.
Always retain an existing better result; one chart occupies at most one slot.

history as an actual empty Top 50. For TUSA, this displayed full chart Pumbility
as gain. The revised v2 method automatically selects a usable personal baseline
or reconstructs an estimated Top 50 from official placements, as specified in
section 5. It never uses missing imported observations as empty score slots.
